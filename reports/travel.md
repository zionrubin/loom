## Travel Vertical Rollup

### 1) Overall status and trajectory
The vertical is generally **stable with mixed operational progress**. On the growth side, the team is actively testing bid expansion in **Brave** to unlock incremental traffic, while also improving reporting automation and partner terms. At the same time, there were a couple of **data/tracking issues** (HLP tracking pause on 2026-06-04 and Pay sales posting issue on 2026-07-02), which were handled quickly but point to ongoing measurement risk. Overall trajectory is **positive but cautious**, with growth initiatives moving forward under close monitoring.

### 2) Main themes discussed
- **Bid optimization / traffic expansion:** On 2026-06-03, Brave bids were planned to increase from ~$2 to **$3 for DT and Mobile**, balancing competitor benchmarks against EPV/EPC and expected traffic lift.
- **Tracking and reporting reliability:** On 2026-06-04, a potential **HLP tracking issue** from Pay led to an immediate test pause. On 2026-06-10, **AXA automation into Tableau** went live, improving reporting flow.
- **Experiment / policy performance analysis:** On 2026-06-08, the team compared **Hostd vs regular funnel** performance after SID exclusion. Early results looked directionally comparable after traffic scaling, but the sample was too small to draw conclusions.
- **Commercial terms / deal management:** On 2026-06-24, the deal rate was reduced from **51% to 46% starting July 1**, with growth expected to justify any future return to higher rates.
- **Sales posting issue:** On 2026-07-02, Pay sales were not posting as expected from **29.06**, but the issue was investigated and resolved.

### 3) Recurring patterns
- **Cautious testing before scaling:** The team repeatedly avoids overcommitting on bids or performance conclusions without enough data.
- **Heavy reliance on tracking quality:** Several updates center on whether data is live, accurate, or automated.
- **Growth tied to commercial leverage:** Better partner terms appear contingent on proving meaningful volume growth.
- **Early-stage reads are treated conservatively:** Hostd results were explicitly flagged as too small a sample to interpret confidently.

### 4) Notable subjects to watch
- **Brave bid test outcomes** after the move to $3: whether traffic meaningfully improves or needs further uplift.
- **HLP / Pay tracking reliability** to ensure no recurring data gaps.
- **Hostd vs regular funnel performance** once more traffic accumulates.
- **Partner deal sustainability** after the 46% rate takes effect on **2026-07-01**.
- **Sales posting recurrence risk** following the 29.06 Pay issue.
