## Life-Insurance Vertical Rollup

### 1) Overall status and trajectory
The vertical trended **from aggressive spend control into more stable, profitability-first execution** over the period **7/19–7/26**. Early discussions centered on rebalancing budget between **Amica** and **Legal**, while later updates show **improved VMM/profitability despite lower scale**. By **7/22–7/23**, performance was generally strong, with **PERM becoming profitable for multiple days** and TERM showing solid pace. Over the weekend (**7/26**), spend was **~15% lower than the prior weekend** while maintaining mostly positive economics, though **Friday VMM was weak** and some delivery issues remain.

### 2) Main themes discussed
- **Spend reallocation and budget pacing:** repeated tuning of daily targets, including shifting budget between **Amica, Legal, and LIGAL**.
- **Quality optimization:** frequent cuts to weaker channels/placements (mobile, weaker Google/desktop placements, seniors segments) to protect VMM.
- **Placement/link strategy:** ongoing debate around **Legal vs. Ladder** placement, old link replacement, and PLI rotation.
- **Campaign-specific control:** repeated focus on **TERM**, **PERM**, **final expense**, and **USAA** weekend activation.
- **Testing and rollout decisions:** side-filters test was closed without implementation; other tests included **Sanity 10–90** product catalog checks and A/B logic for placement ordering.

### 3) Recurring patterns
- **Cut volume, improve profitability:** multiple days showed that reducing spend or scale improved VMM without immediate harm to performance.
- **Quality issues drive action:** weak traffic quality repeatedly triggered changes in chart position, device mix, and bid/target reductions.
- **Weekend sensitivity:** several updates referenced weekend pacing, USAA behavior, and the need to “stay small” or manage trend visibility.
- **Budget shifts between similar campaigns:** Amica/Legal/LIGAL were repeatedly rebalanced as performance moved.
- **Partner/test uncertainty:** several decisions depended on confirming link behavior, auction impact, or partner quality feedback.

### 4) Notable subjects to watch
- **Amica collapse on 7/26**: leads dropped sharply (**12 vs. 66 prior**), suggesting chart/allocation problems that may require immediate correction.
- **Legal/Ladder setup:** still unresolved earlier in the week; the team needs confidence that link swaps or rotation won’t damage outcomes.
- **USAA weekend launch/scale-up:** promising lift, but scaling depends on quality feedback and Doron’s approval.
- **PERM sustainability:** improved and sometimes positive, but should be monitored to ensure cuts don’t suppress future upside.
- **Early-week rebound risk:** Friday’s weak VMM suggests delivery may lag unless TERM/Perm performance holds.
