# pet-insurance — vertical rollup

## Pet-insurance vertical rollup

### 1) Overall status and trajectory
The vertical is in an active optimization phase, with repeated structural changes aimed at improving the mix of **quality vs. profitability**. Since **7/15**, the team has been rolling out campaign restructures and AI Max/Pmax changes, while managing the fallout from learning periods and campaign consolidation. By **7/19–7/22**, the focus had shifted toward stabilizing **Spot** quality, monitoring **Lemonade/PLI** quality, and keeping spend under control as automation and budget allocations evolved.

Overall trajectory is **positive but fragile**: profitability has often improved faster than quality, creating a recurring tension in decision-making.

### 2) Main themes discussed
- **Campaign restructuring and automation**: phased rollout of AI Max, Pmax launches, campaign consolidation, and automation-driven spend management.
- **Quality vs. profitability trade-offs**: especially in **Spot**, **Lemonade**, and **Capsule**, where better profitability sometimes came with weaker lead quality or higher CPA risk.
- **Budget/run-rate management**: discussions around scaling targets, maintaining quality guardrails, and controlling month-end spend.
- **Lemonade/PLI monitoring**: repeated attention to quality dips, with adjustments planned to keep quality within target thresholds.
- **Operational coordination**: process issues such as Partnerize report delivery, handoffs during absence, and an apparent upload/automation issue on **7/22**.

### 3) Recurring patterns
- **Learning periods after changes**: new setups are often introduced with expectation of short-term volatility before performance stabilizes.
- **Quality guardrails are central**: targets like Spot around **400–410**, Lemonade quality **<= 500**, and spend caps are repeatedly referenced.
- **Profitability gains are easier than quality gains**: several updates note that the current improvement is being driven more by efficiency/profitability than by structural quality improvements.
- **Small campaign mix changes can have outsized effects**: trimming LQ, shifting traffic, or moving budget between HQ/LQ sources materially changes outcomes.

### 4) Notable subjects to watch
- **Spot quality deterioration** after new Pmax campaigns in **Capsule/Batter** on **7/19**; team response was to increase MC and trim PPC LQ, but upside may be limited.
- **Lemonade quality volatility** on **7/20–7/22**, despite strong profitability and additional budget, with pressure to end the month below **500** spend.
- **Unexplained Capsule spend spike** of about **$16k** on **7/21**, which may require competitive or measurement diagnosis.
- **Automation / upload reliability** on **7/22**, since spend is now fully automated and any execution issue could affect pacing and quality outcomes.
