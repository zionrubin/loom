## Renters Vertical Rollup

### 1) Overall status and trajectory
The renters vertical is generally **moving forward with expansion and operational cleanup**, but with a clear emphasis on **balancing growth vs. quality/profitability**. On **7/16**, the team aligned on scaling California aggressively and trimming weaker states like **NY/NJ** if needed. By **7/19**, the focus shifted to **quality degradation from an LQ-heavy media mix**, with adjustments made to improve blend quality even at a slight near-term profitability cost.  

Operationally, the integration/testing work for **HostDay/Lemonade** is progressing, though not without friction: the team resolved earlier missing-postback issues, but on **7/20** identified a new lead-submission data loss problem that still blocks clean postback handling. By **7/21**, the ticket had moved into development, suggesting the issue is being actively worked. On **7/22**, expansion continued with **VT and ME** launched, indicating ongoing geographic growth. On **7/26**, most test gaps were said to be resolved, but **automation remains the key unresolved dependency**.

### 2) Main themes discussed
- **Budget scaling and state allocation:** California growth targets were approved, while weaker states (especially NY/NJ) may be reduced.
- **Quality vs. profitability tradeoff:** The media mix was adjusted away from lower-quality campaigns to protect quality.
- **Tracking/integration reliability:** Repeated discussion of **GCLID, postbacks, lead submission, coverage gaps, and automation**.
- **Testing and launch readiness:** Sanity testing, development fixes, and campaign setup updates.
- **Market expansion:** New state launches (**VT, ME**) broadened footprint.

### 3) Recurring patterns
- The team repeatedly favors **growth, but only with tight performance monitoring**.
- There is a recurring need to **rebalance campaign mix** when quality slips.
- **Integration issues** keep surfacing in different forms, especially around **lead-to-postback fidelity** and automation.
- Decisions often hinge on whether a change is **incrementally profitable and operationally safe**.

### 4) Notable subjects to watch
- **California scaling** and whether it sustains the expected win-win for quality and profitability.
- **NY/NJ performance** under the BAKET L segment, since they remain candidates for cuts.
- **HostDay/Lemonade automation**, which is the main remaining blocker for clean conversion visibility.
- **Postback integrity** after lead submission, since this directly affects measurement and campaign reporting.
- **VT/ME performance** after launch, as no early metrics were shared.
