# business-insurance — vertical rollup

## Business-Insurance Rollup

### 1) Overall status and trajectory
The vertical moved from **profitability pressure in mid-July to a clearer recovery by 7/26**. Early signals on **7/12** showed spend stabilization at June levels, but CPC remained elevated and profitability suffered after ChatGPT revenue loss. On **7/15–7/16**, the team focused on **Hartford / General Liability losses** and responded by cutting spend and tightening budget controls. By **7/26**, these actions appeared to pay off: spend dropped **30% (72K → 50.5K)** while revenue fell only **7% (59K)**, pushing **VMM from -9K to +8.4K** and **ROI from -12.5% to +16.5%**. Quality metrics also improved, though some targets remain slightly off.

### 2) Main themes discussed
- **Profitability management:** repeated concern about unprofitable GL-heavy traffic, especially Hartford.
- **Budget and bid optimization:** spend cuts, tighter caps, and a shift toward CPA-based optimization.
- **Traffic quality / mix changes:** removal of low-quality ChatGPT traffic, higher desktop mix, and funnel stabilization.
- **Experiment management:** KDF test launch (**7/21**) and Sticky CTA closure (**7/22**); attention on avoiding overlapping tests.
- **Partner / deal management:** Smart Financial EPC concerns, Progressive status uncertainty, and Auto traffic remaining very low.
- **Reporting / measurement:** a BI discrepancy on **7/26** raised questions about QL count and calculation base.

### 3) Recurring patterns
- **General Liability is the main structural pain point**, repeatedly driving losses and forcing budget reductions.
- **Quality vs profitability tradeoff** appears throughout: some actions improved one metric while hurting another.
- The team frequently responds with **incremental spend cuts and pacing adjustments** rather than broad scaling.
- **Experiment discipline matters**: tests are being managed carefully due to parallel-run constraints and unclear lift.
- **Revenue loss from ChatGPT traffic** shows up repeatedly, but quality concerns persist even after removal.

### 4) Notable subjects to watch
- **Hartford / GL performance**: still the core lever for margins and quality.
- **Progressive test status**: needs confirmation before any conflicting experiments continue.
- **Smart Financial economics**: low EPC despite high volume; deal terms may need revision.
- **BI reporting anomaly on 7/26**: QL/base discrepancy could affect decision-making.
- **CPA targets on Simpli and Hartford**: improving, but not fully clean yet.
